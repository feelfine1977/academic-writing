# Blank manual paper folder

Read [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md) for the complete instructions. Copy `root.md`, `START_HERE.md` and `Cards/` into a new folder under your vault’s `06_Academic_Writing_Lab/Papers/`. Replace every `<paper-key>` with a unique short key and `<paper-title>` with your title.

Alternatively, choose **Papers → Obsidian starter templates** in the app. Its ZIP fills in the keys and title for you and leaves all manuscript fields blank. No LLM is used.
